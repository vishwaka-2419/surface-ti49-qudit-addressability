R9C INTERPRETATION LOCK

Hamiltonian/Pauli-evolution product-formula compilation was tested only as
an approximate structure-aware control.

Within the tested Lie-Trotter and second-order Suzuki-Trotter schedules,
accuracy improves only at a large two-qubit-gate cost.

The best tested approximation reached unitary error norm approximately
7.9e-2 at 146 CX gates.

This is substantially worse than the exact generic synthesis of the
Gray-encoded d=8 Iy(pi/2) rotation, which requires 9 CX gates in the
local all-to-all/linear logical comparison.

Therefore Pauli-product-formula synthesis is not competitive for the
specific single-spin rotation studied here and is retained only as a
negative supplementary benchmark.

No claim is made that all Hamiltonian-aware compilation strategies are
inefficient.
