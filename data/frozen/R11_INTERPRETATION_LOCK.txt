R11 FINAL IBM INTERPRETATION LOCK

Backend: ibm_marrakesh
Physical qubits: [134, 135, 139]
Shots per circuit: 4096
Matched transpiler seeds: 3, 7, 11, 19, 23

Benchmark:
Exact d=8 Iy(pi/2) encoded rotation followed by its inverse,
with a barrier preventing compiler cancellation.

Binary encoding:
mean return probability = 0.848974609375
median native 2Q count = 49
median depth = 185

Gray encoding:
mean return probability = 0.910400390625
median native 2Q count = 18
median depth = 69

Mean paired Gray-minus-binary return probability = 0.06142578125.

Gray outperformed binary for all five matched transpiler seeds.

Median native two-qubit-count reduction = 63.27%.
Median circuit-depth reduction = 62.70%.

INTERPRETATION:
This measurement is an inversion/echo return-probability benchmark,
not a process fidelity or average gate fidelity measurement.

The result demonstrates that the qubit cost of representing the
same d=8 spin rotation depends strongly on encoding. Gray encoding
substantially reduces hardware compilation overhead and produces
a consistently higher measured return probability on the tested
IBM processor.

No claim is made that this constitutes a general optimal qubit
encoding or a direct comparison of native Ti nuclear-gate fidelity
with IBM hardware fidelity.
